How the work lands

What actually changes on an account

Five things I do repeatedly, and the number each one moved. Not testimonial copy — just the mechanics of the job and what they produced.

  • Onboarding

    A rate card matched to real volume

    Every merchant was quoted off the same sheet. Now the card is rebuilt around their actual volume, shipping mix and growth plan — so pricing stops being a negotiation and starts being arithmetic.

    193merchants onboarded end to end
  • Escalation

    Every issue has an owner and a clock

    Escalations used to live in a WhatsApp thread and a spreadsheet. IMAS LITE gives each one a ticket, an assigned owner, an office-hours response clock and an escalation ladder that fires on its own.

    0tickets tracked by hand since
  • Business reviews

    Order data, read monthly

    A per-merchant view of order performance and delivery time, reviewed with the account rather than pushed at them. Problems surface in the numbers before they surface as complaints.

    731accounts on this cadence
  • Retention

    Check in before it breaks

    Proactive follow-up on delayed orders and a clear escalation path, instead of waiting for a merchant to escalate. Renewal conversations open on a track record rather than a pitch.

    85%client retention improvement
  • Expansion

    Deciding where to ship next, on evidence

    Led the push into new regions — reviewing merchant order data to decide which network to open, what volume justifies it, and which existing accounts move first.

    ~25kparcels a month across the portfolio
